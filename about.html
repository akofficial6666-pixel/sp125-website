About Honda SP 125

Introduction

The Honda SP 125 is a stylish and fuel-efficient 125cc commuter motorcycle from Honda. It is designed for riders who want daily reliability, comfortable ergonomics, and modern sporty styling.

Equipped with Honda's eSP (Enhanced Smart Power) technology and PGM-FI, it delivers smooth power and high fuel efficiency, making it ideal for daily college commutes and city travel.

Everyday Use

  • Daily commute to college or workplace
  • Smooth and economical city riding
  • Comfortable upright seating posture for everyday travel
