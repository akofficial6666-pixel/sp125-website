Honda SP 125 Variants

The Honda SP 125 is offered mainly in two variants based on the braking setup, along with a special Sports Edition.

1. Drum Variant

Equipped with 130mm drum brakes on both front and rear wheels with Combi-Brake System (CBS).

  • 130 mm Front & Rear Drum
  • Integrated CBS Braking
  • Full Digital Console
  • Slightly lighter kerb weight (115 kg)

2. Disc Variant

Offers better stopping power with a front disc brake setup while retaining the rear drum brake.

  • 240 mm Front Disc Brake
  • 130 mm Rear Drum Brake with CBS
  • Enhanced braking control and safety
  • Kerb weight: 116 kg

3. Sports Edition

A limited aesthetic edition featuring aggressive graphics and a matte exhaust muffler shield.

  • Distinctive body graphics & stripes
  • Matte finish exhaust cover
  • Body-colored pillion grab rail
  • Available in Disc brake setup
