Honda SP 125 Features

Explore the advanced design, comfort, and safety features of the modern Honda SP 125.

Design & Styling

Features aggressive fuel tank shrouds, sporty graphics, and a sleek chrome muffler cover that give it a premium, modern commuter look.

Display & Controls

Equipped with a fully digital instrument console that displays real-time mileage, average fuel economy, distance-to-empty, and a gear position indicator.

Comfort & Suspension

Offers a long, comfortable seat with a 5-step adjustable rear suspension and telescopic front forks to ensure a smooth ride on uneven city roads.

LED Lighting

Comes with a signature LED DC headlamp that provides constant and bright illumination, making night rides much safer.

Convenience

Includes an integrated engine start/stop switch, silent start with ACG, and a side-stand indicator with an engine cut-off feature for hassle-free everyday utility.

Safety & Braking

Features a Combi-Brake System (CBS) with an Equalizer to distribute braking force efficiently between front and rear wheels, available with an optional front disc brake.
